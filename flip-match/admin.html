Game settings

Shown on the start screen and in the top bar of the game.

Card faces

One image per brand. The board repeats the images to fill every card, and any two cards showing the same image are a match. Square images look best; the whole image is shown, nothing is cropped. Click a tile or drop an image onto it.

Card back, logo & mark

The back fills the whole card (edges may crop). The logo is the start-screen hero; the mark is the small brand logo in the top bar and under "Presented by". Transparent PNGs work best. Defaults come from the WP Gaming DigiCon 2026 deck.

How it works

  • Everything here is saved in this browser only. It is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Open the game from the same address (host and port) as this page, in the same browser, and it will use these cards.
  • To set up another device: Export here, copy the file over, then Import it in the admin on that device.
  • Images are resized to a maximum of 768px and stored as WebP, so uploads stay small.
  • Every round opens with the cards face-up for the preview time, then they flip face-down and shuffle before play starts.
  • Card face colour is the background behind each face image. Keep it dark (the default is the deck's black) so logos with white lettering stay visible.
  • Board size and brand images are separate: 4 images on an 8 × 8 board means each brand appears 16 times, and any two cards with the same brand are a match.
  • Lowering the number of images keeps the extra uploads; Reset to placeholders deletes everything and restores the deck artwork.
  • The game is played with hand gestures through the webcam: hold your hand over a card to flip it. The browser only allows the camera on https:// or localhost, and the hand-tracking model loads from the internet. Touch and mouse keep working if the camera is unavailable.
